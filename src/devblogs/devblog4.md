6 days ago, all my things were in bags and boxes. The next day, I drove 8 hours home. 3 days ago, I began
my CodePlatoon Skillbridge class. Soon, I will be applying for jobs in preparation for the end of my military
career. Life has moved at lightspeed for the past few weeks, and I imagine it won't slow down for quite some time.
Thank you to the friends I made along the way. I hope to see you again in the next chapter.
